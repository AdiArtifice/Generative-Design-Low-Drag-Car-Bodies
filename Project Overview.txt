Project 1 – AI-Driven Aerodynamic Optimization 
Project Generative Design for Low-Drag Car Bodies 
● Goal Use AI (physics-informed generative models) to suggest car body shape 
modifications that improve aerodynamic efficiency (lower drag coefficient). 
● Novelty Integrate CFD simulations with AI models to iteratively refine designs that 
could become an in-house aero design assistant for EV startups. 
● Tech Python, OpenFOAM (CFD), PyTorch, NVIDIA Modulus (physics-informed AI).
